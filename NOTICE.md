are repeated in generated book manifests.

## Generated artifacts

OCR text, modern-English reading layers, normalized page JSON, thumbnails,
scan derivatives, artwork remainder layers, and QA overlays are generated from
the source scans. They may therefore contain source material, OCR mistakes,
and machine-generated interpretation. The repository's MIT License does not
override any rights or restrictions that apply to the underlying scan or its
contents.

Modern text is machine-assisted and is not represented as an authoritative
edition, medical advice, or empirical ground truth. The public reader keeps the
source scan available beside the reading layer so that users can inspect the
evidence directly.
