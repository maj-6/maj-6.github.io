Reading across five centuries

A facsimile that keeps the page—not its barriers.

Each page is translated or normalized independently, with every modern region keyed to its source box and no post-hoc proportional redistribution. The result retains the rhythm of the original while making allocation errors easier to detect and review.

  1. 01

    Recover the page

    Mistral OCR identifies text and layout regions across the full scan, preserving their page coordinates.

  2. 02

    Interpret with restraint

    Language is translated or spelling is normalized while illustrations and the book’s visual voice remain intact.

  3. 03

    Read side by side

    Move instantly between the scan and facsimile, or reveal the OCR layer and region boundaries for comparison.
